Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Duka Ledger is built to be offline-first and local-only. This policy explains exactly what that means.

1. No server, no collection. Duka Ledger has no backend server. Customer names, phone numbers, debts, payments, and business settings are stored exclusively in your device's local browser storage (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded anywhere by this app.

2. Contacts access. If you use "Import from Contacts," your device's contact picker is used only to fill in a name and phone number you choose. The app never reads your full contact list and never stores contacts you don't explicitly import as customers.

3. Notifications. If you enable push notifications, your browser handles this permission directly. We do not use any third-party push or analytics service — notifications are generated entirely on your device.

4. Backups. When you export a backup, a JSON file is created and saved directly to your device through your browser's normal download mechanism. We never see or receive a copy of this file.

5. No tracking. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking scripts of any kind in this app.

6. Deleting your data. Clearing your browser or app storage, or uninstalling the app, permanently deletes all records. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.

7. Contact. Questions about this policy can be sent through the Support Contact details in Settings.

What is Duka Ledger?

Duka Ledger is a free, offline-first credit book for shopkeepers and small business owners who sell on credit. It replaces the paper exercise book — record customers, debts, and payments, print or share professional statements, and never lose a record, even with no internet connection at all.

What it does
✓ Tracks every customer, debt, and payment
✓ Works fully offline — nothing needs the internet
✓ Generates shareable, WhatsApp-ready receipts
✓ Keeps a complete history of every record
✓ Backs up and restores your data as a file you control
✓ Locks with a PIN or fingerprint to protect your books
